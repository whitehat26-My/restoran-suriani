# Getting Restoran Suriani onto Google

Two separate things, often confused:

| | What it is | Who it reaches |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Business Profile** | The Maps listing — photos, hours, reviews, the panel on the right of a search result | Almost everyone looking for food nearby |
| **Search Console** | Tells Google the *website* exists and reports how it is indexed | People who search for the site by name |

For a restaurant, **the Business Profile matters far more than the website.**
Someone searching "nasi ayam hainan Pudu" or "restoran buka 24 jam near me" is
served Maps results, not web pages. The website supports the listing; it does
not replace it.

---

## What I could not do for you, and why

I could not create the Business Profile. It is tied to a Google account and
requires **business verification** — Google sends a postcard, calls the
restaurant's number, or asks for a video walkthrough showing the premises,
signage and kitchen. That verification is a claim of ownership, so it has to
come from you or Rodiah Ahmad's family, not from me. Creating it under any
other identity would be a false ownership claim and could get the listing
suspended.

Search Console verification is the same: it needs a Google account you own.

What I have done is everything up to that line — the images sized to Google's
specs, the structured data, the DNS verification tooling, and the exact steps
below. The parts left for you take about twenty minutes.

**Prerequisite:** steps 2 and 3 below need `restoransuriani.com` registered and
deployed first (see `CLOUDFLARE.md`). Step 1 does not — you can claim the
Business Profile today, and add the website link later.

## 1. Google Business Profile

### ⚠️ Search before you create

A 30-year-old restaurant in Pudu that is already on foodpanda and GrabFood
**almost certainly has a listing already**, auto-generated by Google or added
by a customer. Search Google Maps for "Restoran Suriani Pudu" first.

- **If a listing exists** → open it → **"Claim this business"** or "Own this
  business?" → verify. This keeps the existing reviews, photos and ranking
  history, which are worth a great deal and cannot be transferred.
- **Only create a new listing if there is genuinely none.** Creating a
  duplicate splits your reviews across two listings, and Google may suppress
  both. This is the single most common and most damaging mistake here.

### Details to enter — these must match the website exactly

Google cross-checks your name, address and phone ("NAP") across the web.
Inconsistencies weaken local ranking, so copy these verbatim:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | `Restoran Suriani` |
| Address | `28, Lorong 1/77a, Pudu, 55100 Kuala Lumpur, Wilayah Persekutuan Kuala Lumpur` |
| Phone | `03-9226 1408` |
| Website | `https://restoransuriani.com` |
| Primary category | **Malaysian restaurant** |
| Secondary categories | Halal restaurant *(see the caveat below)*, Hainanese restaurant, Restaurant |
| Hours | **Open 24 hours**, all seven days |
| Opened | 1995 |
| Attributes | Dine-in, Takeaway, Delivery (if foodpanda/GrabFood are live) |

⚠️ **On the "Halal restaurant" category:** only add it if the restaurant holds
a current JAKIM certificate. You told me it does not. Google's category is not
a legal claim in the way a badge on your own website is, but it is still a
representation to customers, and Malaysian consumers reasonably read it as
certification. My recommendation is to leave it off and let the bismillah on
your signboard and the Malay menu speak for themselves — the same call the
website makes.

### Photos to upload

Ready in `public/assets/google/`:

| File | Size | Upload as |
|---|---|---|
| `logo.jpg` | 720×720 | **Logo** |
| `cover.jpg` | 1080×608 | **Cover photo** — the signboard, cropped to fill |
| `interior.jpg` | 1080×810 | Photo → Interior |
| `dining-area.jpg` | 1080×810 | Photo → Interior |

These are re-crops of your originals, framed and sized to Google's specs. The
originals are portrait phone photos, which Google letterboxes or crops badly.

**Photos are the highest-leverage thing you can improve.** Listings with food
photos get materially more clicks and direction requests than listings without.
You currently have none — four or five well-lit dish photos (nasi ayam Hainan,
nasi lemak, a masakan panas spread, ayam masak merah) would do more for you
than any other single change, on Google and on the website both. Shoot them in
daylight near the front of the shop, from slightly above, with the plate
filling the frame.

### After verification

- Turn on **messaging** only if someone will actually watch it. An ignored

- Add the **menu link**: `https://restoransuriani.com/#menu`
- Ask regulars to leave reviews, and reply to every one. Reply rate is a
  visible signal to customers and a modest ranking factor.
- Add the foodpanda and GrabFood links under "Ordering links" once you send me
  those URLs for the website.

## 2. Google Search Console

Needs the domain registered and the site deployed.

1. <https://search.google.com/search-console> → **Add property** → choose
   **Domain** (not "URL prefix" — Domain covers apex, `www` and every
   subdomain at once, and cannot be broken by a redeploy).
2. Enter `restoransuriani.com`. Google shows a TXT record like
   `google-site-verification=abc123…`.
3. Publish it with the token part only:
   ```sh
   ./scripts/cloudflare-setup.sh search-console abc123...
   ```
4. Click **Verify** in Search Console. DNS can take a few minutes.
5. **Sitemaps** → submit `sitemap.xml`.
6. **URL Inspection** → paste `https://restoransuriani.com/` → **Request
   indexing**.

Leave that verification TXT record published — Google re-checks it, and
removing it un-verifies the property.

### Then check two things

- **URL Inspection → Test Live URL** must succeed. If it fails, Bot Fight Mode
  is challenging Googlebot — see the warning in `CLOUDFLARE.md`.
- **Rich Results Test** (<https://search.google.com/test/rich-results>) should
  report a valid `Restaurant` item, picking up the hours, address, phone and
  the three photo ratios.

## 3. What is already in place

- **`Restaurant` structured data** in `public/index.html` — address, 24/7
  opening hours, phone, cuisines, price range, menu link, and images at 1:1,
  4:3 and 16:9, which is the set Google asks for in rich results.
- **Open Graph card** (`public/assets/og-image.jpg`, 1200×630) for WhatsApp,
  Facebook and Google previews. This fixed a real bug: the previous `og:image`
  was one of the portrait photos, so previews cropped to a band of sky with
  the signboard out of frame.
- **`sitemap.xml`** and a `robots.txt` that allows everything, including AI
  crawlers — for a restaurant found by search, being absent from AI answers is
  a lost customer rather than a security win.
- **Canonical URL** and a `www → apex` redirect, so Google sees one address
  rather than two competing copies.

Regenerate the images after replacing any photo:

```sh
npm i -D playwright && node scripts/make-images.mjs
```
